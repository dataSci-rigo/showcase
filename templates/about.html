How the portal works

This is a single door to several personal projects. Pick an app from the home grid and the portal serves it at this same address; the ⌂ Portal button in the bottom bar always brings you back. The bar also shows a live percentage of the AI budget this demo has used — a few of the apps call Claude behind the scenes, and every call is metered.

Guest accounts are read-mostly by design: anything destructive or credential-touching is blocked at the door, and some apps run in their own demo/guest modes on top of that.

Omaha Poker vs AI

Heads-up Fixed-Limit Omaha Hi/Lo (8-or-better) against a poker agent trained from scratch by self-play with Deep CFR (counterfactual regret minimization with neural networks). No hand-coded strategy: everything it knows it learned from playing itself.

You get four hole cards; the best high hand and best qualifying low hand split the pot. Stacks reset to a fixed baseline every hand — the session score under "Hand over" is what tracks who's winning. There's also a "vs Friend" mode: share the page URL with ?seat=1.

Just press Start Game — no extra login, and this app uses no AI-API budget (the agent runs locally).

Arcade

A collection of kids' learning games: math (Math Smash, Math Garden), spelling and phonics with real audio, letter drawing, memory match, mazes and more. Sound games speak words out loud; the letter-drawing game sends your child's drawing to an AI model that recognizes the letter.

Best on a tablet or touchscreen. The AI-powered games (letter recognition, vocabulary) count against the budget bar.

AI Prep

Technical-interview practice. A dashboard with multiple-choice quizzes (data structures & algorithms, ML system design), progress tracking with accuracy rings, and LeetCode Live: take a real problem, write pseudocode, and get an AI review of your approach.

Quiz answers are checked instantly; the pseudocode review calls Claude and counts against the budget bar.

Compass

A 7-Habits-style weekly planner: a mission statement, life roles with goals, weekly "big rocks", day-by-day scheduled tasks with priorities and estimates, and end-of-week reviews with computed stats and an AI-written analysis of wins, concerns and patterns.

The demo shows a curated, always-current showcase: an in-flight week plus two finished weeks of history — seeded into a separate database, so it's fully isolated and read-only.

On its login page, type demo in the password box to start the read-only tour. "Sign out" exits the demo.

Praxis

A personal deliberate-practice system built on three books: Mindset (Dweck), Flow (Csikszentmihalyi) and Grit (Duckworth). Practice sessions are captured through a Telegram bot during the day; this website is the review side — analytics, streaks and reflections on the practice log.

The demo login is printed right on its welcome box: username guest, password demo. Read-only.

Fridge Recipes

"What can I cook with what I have?" Stock a virtual fridge with your ingredients and get matching recipes, including near-misses showing what one more ingredient would unlock, plus AI-suggested substitutions.

The portal drops you straight into demo mode with its own per-browser fridge — play freely, nothing you do touches real data.

Donor Research

A research tool over California campaign-finance public records (CAL-ACCESS and NetFile filings): search donors, see their giving history across candidates and committees with estimates vs. actuals, browse candidates and PACs, and check filing deadlines.

Guests use a read-only instance: the underlying databases can't be modified, and the external enrichment lookups (LinkedIn, RocketReach, web search, AI questions) are allowed but capped by a shared usage meter.

Start at the donor search on the main page — the dataset is real public-record data, so try any California donor name.
